My Connection to the Lotus Car Brand

on October 31, 2026 by Philip
All Posts

The car brand Lotus was like a gem in the rubble for me, i love sports cars and supercars like Ferrari, Lamborghini, Koenigsegg, but they are all really expensive and super hard to get, especially Koenigsegg since they only make around 3 a year!

Why I've dedicated myself to buying one of these cars

When i found a Lotus for sale on the Facebook market place i thought my eyes where deceiving me, a sports car for only around 38k (and upwards depending on the models and class) which is still a lot but very cheap if we look at other brands like Corvette and Dodge.

They also have an interesting history around where they come from, origninating from Britain, the car brand was formed when Colin Chapman made the first lotus car and was then transformed into the company and then the brand became built off the philosophies of lightweight construction and aerodynamics, said as "simplify, then add lightness".

some other reasons i want a Lotus

  • They are very light weight
  • They semi unknown so itll be an eyegrabber
  • They are semi affordable.-not really but i plan on saving my money and then splitting the bill with my parents.
  • They are very stylish
  • they are very interesting cars
